02 — The Collection

Waapsi  वापसी

A Return

Waapsi — वापसी, meaning return — is Michri’s ode to coming home. To the objects, rituals and textures that make a space feel like yours again; to lazy afternoons that stretch without guilt, and textiles chosen with care and passed down without question.

Drawing on the vocabulary of Indian architecture — jalis, aangan, and the quiet geometry of everyday craft — the collection borrows simple lines from Athangudi tiling and earthy tones drawn from oxide floors. Not nostalgia. Not heritage. Reconnection — for someone who has travelled the world and still chooses the familiar, but with new eyes.
